## What we're trying to do

A few big things, mostly.

### Build a local network of AI, ML, and robotics people.

Cache Valley should be a hub of innovation in this space, and that doesn't happen unless the right people end up in the same rooms. So this is a deliberate effort to put them there.

### Help local orgs retrofit their intelligence for the AI age, human-first.

We want to be ambassadors for AI as a performance multiplier, not a reaper of jobs. There's a way to do this that's good for the people doing the work, and we'd like Cache Valley orgs to land on the good version of it.

### Help people find jobs in the field, or move into AI roles where they already work.

This is an emerging field and very few people locally know what's really going on. The transition is going to happen one way or the other, so it's better if there's a critical local voice involved in how it lands here.

### Meet up in real life, in the actual world.

Most of what's interesting about AI is happening online. Most of what's interesting about Cache Valley isn't.

## A few rules of the road

### Use your human voice.

This group is for humans to learn and connect with other humans. Maybe someday we'll set up an agent-first Discord. For now though, when you talk to the group, talk like you. AI summaries of events and skill docs when you're sharing a project are great. But if this group turns into clankers talking to clankers, the value evaporates for everyone.

### Live dangerously.

Throw in some typos so we know we're getting your thoughts and not ChatGPT's. Who knows, you might even like it.

### Strong opinions welcome.

We expect this group to have a wide range of strong strong feelings about AI, from "End of Times" to "utopia for humans" and everything in between. Part of why we're here is to help sort through where this all actually lands, so don't dilute your views. JT and Dan, who started the group, tend to land somewhere in the middle, but the discourse itself isn't something to shy away from. Can't leave all the philosophizing to the bots.

### High trust, but in the open.

This is a high-trust group. We share what we're working on, and we need to be able to do that with a modicum of discretion.

That said, we're firm believers in building in the open. When anyone can spin up whatever tool or project we're working on in a weekend, the moat shrinks down to quality of human thought, insight, and speed to execution. Building in the open means more to gain than to lose.
