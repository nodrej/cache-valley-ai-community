FAQ

The questions people actually ask.

Do I need to be technical to come?

Not at all. The room is intentionally a mix of engineers, students, educators, founders, and curious neighbors. Being interested is enough.

How much does it cost?

The community is free. Lunch meetups happen at local spots, so you order and pay for your own food — no tickets, no cover.

Is there a schedule of talks?

No slides, no keynote. It's lunch and conversation. If someone wants to demo something on their laptop, great — but that's a sidebar, not the main event.

What if I can't make it in person?

Hop into the Discord. Most of the conversation, links, and recaps live there between meetups.

Who runs this?

Locals in Cache Valley who wanted a friendlier, more honest place to talk about AI than what was already online. Inspired by the local tech meetups already thriving on cachevalley.co.
