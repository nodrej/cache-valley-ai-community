Community

It's a small room, not a private club.

AI is changing fast enough that none of us can really keep up with it alone. So this is a small room of people in Cache Valley comparing notes. No conference, no membership, no gatekeeping. Sometimes the conversations turn into side projects. Sometimes they just turn into better questions to ask.

  • 01

    Engineers, founders, makers

    Bring your experiments, your strong opinions about tools, and whatever thing you've been stuck on this week.

  • 02

    Students and educators

    Bring your questions. We're all still figuring out what's worth knowing and what's noise.

  • 03

    Curious neighbors

    If AI feels like something happening "out there" and you'd like it to make a little more sense, come hang out.

FAQ

The questions people actually ask.

Do I need to be technical to come?

Nope. The room is on purpose a mix of engineers, students, educators, founders, and people who just want to understand what's going on. Being interested is enough.

How much does it cost?

It's free to join. Lunch meetups happen at local spots, so you just order and pay for your own food. No tickets, no cover.

Is there a schedule of talks?

No slides, no keynote. It's just lunch and conversation. If somebody wants to pull up a demo on their laptop, great, but that's a sidebar, not the main event.

What if I can't make it in person?

Hop into the Discord. Most of the conversation lives there between meetups anyway.

Who runs this?

A few of us in Cache Valley who wanted a more honest, less online place to talk about AI. Inspired by the other local tech meetups already going on at cachevalley.co.
